# Defence set 2 (2026-09-16): Mortar, Tesla Coil, Freeze Tower, Minigun, Laser Gate

Five new props for `ServerStorage.Builds/Defences` of the New Map Cucumber Game, built with
`defenselib.py` exactly like the first set (read `STYLE.md` first: units, +Y facing, the
`build(D)` module shape, the hard rules, the API). They are a MATCHED PACK with a look of
their own, taken from the reference renders the user supplied: chunky low-poly toy-fortress,
**dark charcoal grey bodies with bevelled/chamfered edges, one accent colour per prop, hex
bolt heads on the bases, octagonal tiered plinths.** They must sit next to each other and
read as one family.

## Palette for THIS set (hex, no `#`)

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Body dark | `45474d` | main charcoal, big masses |
| Body mid | `565962` | the next tier / band touching a dark part (adjacent parts must differ in value) |
| Body light | `6a6e78` | small raised details on mid parts |
| Bolt / bore | `2f3136` | hex bolt heads, recesses, the inside of a barrel |
| Ice | `9ed6ff` | Freeze Tower crystals and icicles (SmoothPlastic) |
| Ice glow | `bfe4ff` | Freeze Tower orb (`rbx_material="Neon"`) |
| Laser red | `ff2f2f` | Laser Gate beams and emitter cores (Neon) |
| Tan | `c8b276` | Minigun / Mortar accents (corner blocks, pivot discs, muzzle ring, ammo belt) |
| Tesla blue | `4f7fc2` | Tesla Coil corner blocks, coil bands, prong tips |
| Tesla glow | `35a7ff` | Tesla Coil neon ring cores (Neon) |
| Tesla orb | `7fb3e8` | Tesla Coil sphere (`rbx_material="Metal"`, smooth=True) |

Hex bolt head = `D.cyl(bm, (x, y, z0), (x, y, z0 + 0.22), 0.24, segs=6)` (or along the face
normal for a bolt on a vertical face), colour `2f3136`. Corner blocks = `D.beveled_box`
with `bevel=0.14`, sitting on the base's diagonal corners, each with one bolt on its outer
face.

## Shared rules

- Footprint inside the 8 x 8 tile (x, y in [-4, 4]); nothing below z = 0; height <= 10.
- Face **+Y** (barrels, emitters, the "front"). Yaw / pitch groups are modelled at zero.
- Tri budget per prop is in its section. Check `D.report(COLLECTION)["tris"]`.
- Part names: distinct, no spaces, prefixed by group where a runtime rig needs it
  (`Base*` static, `Head*` yaws about world Z through (0, 0), `Spin*` rolls about the
  barrel axis, `Tube*` pitches about the trunnion, `Orb` spins in place, `Beam1..4`).
- Export `NOTES` (what the installer / DefenceService must know: sizes, rig groups, axes,
  what glows) and `PIVOTS` (Roblox-relevant points in the prop's own frame, Blender
  coordinates: the installer converts). `STATES` where a pose angle matters.
- Run headless from the `defenses` folder (never save the .blend; several props build in
  parallel from it):

      & "C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 4.5\blender.exe" -b defenses.blend --python run_headless.py -- build_<prop>.py <Collection> <yaw> <pitch> <margin>

  It prints `REPORT {...}` (parts, tris, size_studs, min_z, per_part), writes
  `renders/<Collection>.png` (three-quarter from yaw/pitch, default 35/22) and
  `renders/<Collection>_eye.png`, exports `fbx/<Collection>.fbx` and
  `fbx/<Collection>.manifest.json`. Look at BOTH renders (Read the PNGs) and iterate until
  the prop matches its section; then stop.

## 1. FreezeTower  (`build_freeze_tower.py`, COLLECTION "FreezeTower", budget 1700 tris)

A stone rook: a chunky charcoal tower with ice growing on it, ~7 wide, ~9.5 tall.

- **Base**: two octagonal tiers, `D.prism(D.ngon_pts(8, r, phase=pi/8), z0, z1)`:
  tier 1 r 3.5, z 0..1.2 (body dark); tier 2 r 2.7, z 1.2..2.0 (body mid); a lip r 2.1,
  z 2.0..2.5 (body dark). The octagon's flat faces face the axes (phase pi/8).
- **Ice on the base**: 6 crystal clusters around tier 1 (on its top surface near the edge,
  some leaning over the side): each cluster = 2-3 faceted crystals, a big one and a small
  one, made with `D.spike(bm, base, tip, r, segs=6, tip_r=0.08)` r 0.35..0.55, length
  0.9..1.6, tilted outward 10-25 deg (`D.aim`), colour Ice `9ed6ff` SmoothPlastic. One
  part "IceCrystals" for all of them (one bmesh). Also 2 flat ice "sheets" draped over the
  tier-1 top edge (thin beveled boxes 1.4 x 1.0 x 0.18 with an icicle hanging off the edge).
- **Column**: `D.cyl` r 1.5, z 2.5..6.6, segs 12, body dark ("Column").
- **Band**: an 8-gon ring r 1.95 z 3.9..4.7 (body mid, "Band") with 3 icicles hanging under
  it (spikes pointing DOWN, r 0.28, length 1.3, Ice) around it ("BandIcicles").
- **Collar**: an 8-gon prism r 2.1 z 6.6..7.3 (body mid, "Collar") + a 12-seg disc r 1.7
  z 7.3..7.6 (body dark, "CollarTop") with 2 icicles hanging from its rim (Ice).
- **Prongs**: 4 claws at 0 / 90 / 180 / 270 deg at radius 1.45, each a `D.beveled_box`
  0.85 x 0.85 in plan, z 7.6..9.3, leaning inward ~8 deg (rot about the tangent), body
  dark ("Prongs", one part); two of them carry an ice cap on top (small Ice spike, "ProngIce").
- **Orb**: `D.ico(bm, (0, 0, 8.55), 1.0, subdiv=1)` Ice glow `bfe4ff` **Neon** ("Orb") -
  faceted, sits between the prongs, its bottom clear of CollarTop.
- `PIVOTS = {"OrbCentre": (0, 0, 8.55)}`. NOTES: the freeze aura is cast from OrbCentre;
  "Orb" spins; ice parts are Ice/SmoothPlastic, the orb is Neon.

## 2. LaserGate  (`build_laser_gate.py`, COLLECTION "LaserGate", budget 1500 tris)

Two identical charcoal pillars with four red laser beams strung between them across the
gap; enemies walk THROUGH the gap along Y. Total width 8.0 (x in [-4, 4]), depth 2.4,
height 6.4.

- **Pillars** at x = -2.9 (prefix `PillarL`) and x = +2.9 (`PillarR`), each:
  - flared foot: an 8-gon frustum `D.cyl(a, b, 1.25, segs=8, r2=1.0)` z 0..1.1 is too
    round - instead `D.prism` of a 2.2 x 2.2 square (phase pi/4 ngon 4) z 0..1.1 for the
    plinth plus a `D.wedge`-style chamfer is fine; simplest good read: `D.beveled_box`
    2.2 x 2.4 (x by y) x 1.1 tall with bevel 0.3 (body dark, "PillarLFoot") and 2 hex
    bolts on its front (+Y) face and 1 on the outer face.
  - column: `D.beveled_box` 1.7 x 1.7 (plan) z 1.1..5.5, bevel 0.12, body mid
    ("PillarLColumn").
  - a recessed panel on the OUTER face (away from the gap): a beveled box 0.9 wide x 0.12
    deep x 2.8 tall standing 0.06 proud, body dark ("PillarLPanel").
  - cap: `D.beveled_box` 2.1 x 2.1 x 0.7, z 5.5..6.2, bevel 0.28, body dark ("PillarLCap")
    with a smaller flat top slab 1.5 x 1.5 x 0.2 z 6.2..6.4 body mid ("PillarLCapTop").
  - four emitters on the INNER face (facing the other pillar), at z 2.0, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0:
    each a dark rim `D.cyl` r 0.42 along X, 0.3 long, proud of the column face by 0.25
    (`2f3136`, all four in "PillarLEmitters") and a red Neon core `D.cyl` r 0.26, 0.12
    long, on the rim's face ("PillarLCores", Neon `ff2f2f`).
- **Beams**: four `D.cyl` r 0.07 along X from the left core face to the right core face
  (x -1.95 .. 1.95) at z 2.0 / 3.0 / 4.0 / 5.0, Neon `ff2f2f`, ONE PART EACH named
  "Beam1" .. "Beam4" (bottom to top) - the runtime toggles / measures them.
- `PIVOTS = {"BeamLeft": (-1.95, 0, 3.5), "BeamRight": (1.95, 0, 3.5)}`. NOTES: the
  damage plane is the x-z rectangle between the pillars (x in [-1.95, 1.95], z 0..5.5);
  enemies cross it along Y; Beam1..4 are Neon parts to flicker.
- Tip: build one pillar with a helper `pillar(sign, prefix)` called twice.

## 3. Minigun  (`build_minigun.py`, COLLECTION "Minigun", budget 2000 tris)

A squat charcoal rotary-gun turret with TAN accents, ~7 x 7 footprint, 5.6 tall, six
barrels pointing +Y.

- **Base** (`Base*` static): an 8-gon plate r 3.7 z 0..0.9 (body dark, "BasePlate", phase
  pi/8 so flats face the axes); four TAN corner blocks `D.beveled_box` 1.7 x 1.2 x 1.05 tall
  placed on the four diagonal corners (centre radius 3.0 at 45 / 135 / 225 / 315 deg,
  rotated to sit flush with the octagon's diagonal faces, `rot=D.rot_euler(rz=45)`
  etc.), z 0.05..1.1 ("BaseCorners", Tan) each with one hex bolt on its outer face
  ("BaseBolts", `2f3136`); a 12-gon collar r 2.3 z 0.9..1.7 (body mid, "BaseCollar") with
  4 hex bolts on its top rim at 45-deg positions.
- **Head** (`Head*` yaws about Z through (0, 0)): pedestal `D.cyl` r 1.55 z 1.7..2.5 (body
  dark, "HeadPedestal"); the YOKE: a thick upright plate on the LEFT of the gun,
  beveled box x -1.25..-0.55, y -0.9..0.9, z 2.5..4.9, bevel 0.15, body mid ("HeadYoke"),
  with a TAN pivot disc `D.cyl` r 0.6 along X from x -1.45 to -1.25 at (y 0, z 4.1)
  ("HeadPivotDisc"); the RECEIVER: beveled box x -0.55..1.35, y -2.0..0.9, z 3.2..5.0, bevel
  0.18, body dark ("HeadReceiver"); a carry HANDLE on top: two small posts 0.25 x 0.25 x
  0.35 at y -1.6 and y -0.4 plus a bar 0.28 x 1.5 x 0.25 spanning them at z 5.35..5.6
  (body mid, "HeadHandle"); the AMMO BOX hanging on the left: beveled box x -3.0..-1.5,
  y -1.7..-0.2, z 1.75..3.3 body dark ("HeadAmmoBox") with a TAN lid beveled box
  x -3.05..-1.45, y -1.75..-0.15, z 3.3..3.65 ("HeadAmmoLid"); the AMMO BELT: 7 TAN links
  (`D.beveled_box` 0.5 x 0.32 x 0.26, bevel 0.06) stepping in an arc from the lid top at
  (-2.25, -0.95, 3.75) up and right to the receiver's left port at (-0.75, -0.95, 4.45),
  each link rotated to follow the arc ("HeadBelt", one part); a small dark port box on the
  receiver's left face where the belt enters ("HeadPort", body mid).
- **Spin** (`Spin*` rolls about the barrel axis, which is parallel to +Y through
  (x 0.45, z 4.1)): SIX barrels `D.cyl` r 0.19, segs 8, from y 0.9 to y 4.3, on a ring of
  radius 0.5 around the axis (60-deg spacing) ("SpinBarrels", one part, body light
  `6a6e78`); a rear housing `D.cyl` r 0.9 along Y from y 0.6 to 1.5 (body mid,
  "SpinHousing"); a TAN muzzle ring: 12-gon prism r 0.78, from y 3.35 to 3.9, hollow look
  not required ("SpinMuzzleRing"); the barrels stick 0.4 past the ring.
- `PIVOTS = {"YawPivot": (0, 0, 1.7), "BarrelAxis": (0.45, 0, 4.1), "MuzzleTip": (0.45, 4.3, 4.1)}`.
  NOTES: Head* yaws, Spin* rolls about BarrelAxis (a line parallel to +Y), muzzle flash at
  MuzzleTip, tracer colour tan/yellow.

## 4. Mortar  (`build_mortar.py`, COLLECTION "Mortar", budget 1600 tris)

A stubby charcoal mortar on a low hex base with TAN accents, tube raised 55 deg toward +Y.
~6.8 x 6.8 footprint, ~7.2 tall to the muzzle.

- **Base** (`Base*`): 8-gon plate r 3.4 z 0..0.9 (body dark, "BasePlate", phase pi/8);
  four TAN corner blocks like the Minigun's (radius 2.75, 1.5 x 1.1 x 1.0 tall, one bolt
  each; "BaseCorners" + "BaseBolts"); an inner 12-gon platform r 2.2 z 0.9..1.3 (body mid,
  "BasePlatform").
- **Mount** (`Mount*` yaws about Z): turntable `D.cyl` r 1.9 z 1.3..1.7 (body dark,
  "MountTurntable"); two TRUNNION CHEEKS, one each side of the tube: each a `D.prism` in
  plan of a 0.45-thick plate? No - build each cheek as a beveled box x [1.25, 1.75] (and
  mirrored), y -1.35..1.05, z 1.7..3.4 (body mid, "MountCheeks") topped by a narrower
  upright beveled box y -0.6..0.6, z 3.4..4.7 (body mid, same part) so the cheek steps in
  toward the trunnion; TAN trunnion discs `D.cyl` r 0.62 along X from x 1.75 to 2.0 (and
  -1.75 to -2.0) at (y 0, z 4.0) ("MountTrunnionDiscs"); a crossbar between the cheeks at
  the back: beveled box x -1.25..1.25, y -1.3..-0.9, z 1.7..2.5 (body dark, "MountBar");
  2 hex bolts on each cheek's front lower face ("MountBolts").
- **Tube** (`Tube*` pitches about the trunnion axis = a line parallel to X through
  (0, 0, 4.0)): direction `d = (0, cos 55deg, sin 55deg)`. Main tube `D.cyl` from
  `T - d*1.1` to `T + d*3.5` (T = (0, 0, 4.0)), r 0.95, segs 12, body dark ("TubeBarrel");
  two rings `D.cyl` r 1.07, 0.32 long, centred at `T + d*0.6` and `T + d*2.4` (body mid,
  "TubeRings"); a muzzle collar `D.cyl` r 1.12, 0.45 long, ending exactly at the muzzle
  (body mid, "TubeMuzzle"); the BORE: a dark disc `D.cyl` r 0.72, 0.1 long, recessed 0.05
  inside the muzzle face (`2f3136`, "TubeBore") so the muzzle reads hollow; a breech cap
  `D.cyl` r 0.8 0.3 long at the back end (body mid, "TubeBreech").
- `PIVOTS = {"YawPivot": (0, 0, 1.3), "PitchPivot": (0, 0, 4.0), "MuzzleTip": <T + d*3.5>}`,
  `STATES = {"Elevation": 55}`. NOTES: shells leave MuzzleTip; Mount* yaws, Tube* pitches
  about PitchPivot (axis parallel to X); built at 55 deg elevation.

## 5. TeslaCoil  (`build_tesla_coil.py`, COLLECTION "TeslaCoil", budget 1800 tris)

A charcoal coil tower with BLUE accents: octagonal base with blue corner blocks, a column
wearing two glowing blue coil bands, a crown of four prongs around a metallic blue orb.
~7.4 x 7.4 footprint, ~9.4 tall.

- **Base**: 8-gon plate r 3.7 z 0..1.0 (body dark, "BasePlate", phase pi/8); four BLUE
  corner blocks (`4f7fc2`, radius 3.0, 1.7 x 1.2 x 1.05 tall, one bolt each; "BaseCorners"
  + "BaseBolts"); a second 8-gon tier r 2.8 z 1.0..1.6 (body mid, "BaseTier"); an inner
  12-gon ring r 2.15 z 1.6..2.0 (body dark, "BaseRing").
- **Column**: `D.cyl` r 1.5 z 2.0..6.6 segs 12 (body dark, "Column").
- **Coil bands** at z 3.2 and z 4.4: each a 12-gon prism r 2.05 z h..h+0.75 BLUE
  ("CoilBands", one part for both) with a NEON inner ring: a 12-gon prism r 2.1
  z h+0.26..h+0.49 Tesla glow `35a7ff` Neon ("CoilGlow", one part for both) - the neon
  ring stands 0.05 proud of the blue band so it reads as a lit groove.
- **Cap**: 12-gon prism r 1.95 z 6.6..7.3 (body mid, "Cap") + disc r 1.45 z 7.3..7.65 (body
  dark, "CapTop").
- **Prongs**: 4 claws at 45 / 135 / 225 / 315 deg (between the axes, so the front view
  shows two) at radius 1.35, each `D.beveled_box` 0.75 x 0.75 plan, z 7.65..9.35, leaning
  inward ~7 deg (body dark, "Prongs") with a BLUE tip block 0.85 x 0.85 x 0.35 on top of
  each ("ProngTips").
- **Orb**: stem `D.cyl` r 0.28 z 7.65..8.35 (body mid, "OrbStem"); `D.uvsphere(bm, (0, 0,
  8.95), 0.85, segs=12, rings=8)` Tesla orb `7fb3e8`, `rbx_material="Metal"`, `smooth=True`
  ("Orb").
- `PIVOTS = {"OrbCentre": (0, 0, 8.95), "ProngTip1": (0.95, 0.95, 9.5), "ProngTip2":
  (-0.95, 0.95, 9.5), "ProngTip3": (-0.95, -0.95, 9.5), "ProngTip4": (0.95, -0.95, 9.5)}`
  (put the real tip coordinates you end up with). NOTES: lightning Beams run OrbCentre ->
  ProngTip*, then to the zombie; CoilGlow is Neon.
